Privacy

This site stores the following items in your browser's storage:

  • Theme: remembers whether you last used light or dark mode.
  • Video sound: remembers whether you muted videos, and at what volume.
  • Slideshow speed: remembers how many seconds each photo is shown.
  • Captions: remembers whether you turned lightbox captions off, until you close the browser tab.
  • Site ID: tracks the current site build so stale cached data is automatically cleared when the site is updated.

All values stay in your browser and are never transmitted anywhere. No personal data is collected, no analytics are run, and no third-party tracking is used.

To clear all stored data and return the site to its default state, visit ?clear.
